## Format choices

- Vertical delivery: 9:16; 3:4 is also used in selected design and illustration briefs.
- Product detail: 1:1.
- General editorial and education: 16:9.
- Wider narrative: 2:1 or 21:9.
- Existing footage: preserve its supported source ratio.

These are creative choices. Confirm the actual provider's allowed values before submitting. Do not silently substitute an unsupported crop or duration.

## Reduce the first test

For unstable identity, start with a blink and a fixed camera. For hand failures, use one object and one contact. For typography, first test a stationary short phrase. For audio, begin with one voice and one short sentence. For continuation, compare the source's last frame and sound with the new clip's first frame and sound.
