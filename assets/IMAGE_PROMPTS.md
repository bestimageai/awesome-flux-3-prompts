## Cover — The lantern relay

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide repository cover, one coherent cinematic scene
Primary request: a static concept frame for a quiet coastal science-fiction short about a practical lantern signal
Scene: an older lighthouse keeper in an olive knitted coat stands on a stone balcony above a dark harbor, setting one rectangular amber lantern onto the parapet; a single distant window shows a small warm light
Style: grounded cinematic production art, photorealistic materials, restrained atmosphere
Composition: wide establishing view from behind and beside the keeper at shoulder height, readable lantern and distant harbor, no montage or panel divisions
Lighting: cool evening ambience, warm lantern reflection on damp stone
Constraints: one keeper, one lantern, plausible hands and balcony, no futuristic skyline, spacecraft, weapons, readable text, logos or watermark
Output aspect ratio: 21:9.
```
