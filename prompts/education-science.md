# FLUX 3 education and science prompts

Six visual explanations with small, reviewable learning goals. Check the final scientific content and generated labels before teaching; these are proposed scenes, not experiments performed by the maintainers.

<a id="l01"></a>
## L01 — Move the card, not the light

**Use:** introductory shadow demonstration · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** card shape, retaining fixed light and screen.  
**Localization:** canonical source for I18N-03.

```text
Create a simple classroom demonstration. A small lamp on the left shines toward a vertical white screen on the right. One opaque square card stands between them on a short handle. Fixed side camera shows lamp, card and shadow together.
00:00–00:05 — Hold the initial arrangement. One adult hand grips the card handle without blocking the lamp.
00:05–00:10 — Move the card slowly closer to the lamp, along the line between lamp and screen. Its shadow on the screen becomes larger. The lamp and screen do not move.
00:10–00:15 — Stop and hold the new arrangement. The hand keeps the card steady.
NARRATION: In clear English, say exactly: "The light stays here. Moving the card closer to the light makes its shadow larger."
AUDIO: Quiet classroom beneath the narration; no music.
TEXT: None.
LOCK: One lamp, one card, one screen, constant brightness and a single consistent shadow.
AVOID: shadow shrinking during the move, multiple lights, floating card, changing screen, extra labels, logos or watermark.
```

## L02 — The paper filter

**Use:** visible separation process · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 20 seconds · **Adjust:** vessel size; do not imply drinking-water treatment.

```text
Illustrate filtration with a transparent funnel containing folded filter paper above an empty clear beaker. A second beaker holds water mixed with coarse clean sand. This is a classroom model, not a drinking-water purification demonstration.
00:00–00:06 — Locked side view. An adult pours a small amount of the mixture slowly into the lined funnel, keeping the rim and receiving beaker visible.
00:06–00:14 — Liquid passes through the paper into the receiving beaker. Sand remains visibly on the paper; flow is modest, not instantaneous.
00:14–00:20 — Stop pouring and hold on the retained sand and collected liquid.
NARRATION: Exact English: "The paper holds back these sand particles. Clearer water is not necessarily safe to drink."
AUDIO: Small pouring sound and quiet room tone below the voice.
LOCK: Same two beakers, funnel, paper position and plausible liquid volumes.
AVOID: drinking, removing all contaminants, magical color change, extra vessels, boiling, safety certification, labels, logos or watermark.
```

## L03 — Hear the ending

**Use:** English listening and pronunciation · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 18 seconds · **Adjust:** teaching background, not the exact word sequence.

```text
An adult language teacher sits against a plain warm background with two large picture cards: a cap and a cat. Use original simple illustrations and no printed words.
00:00–00:06 — Medium view. Raise the cap card and say in neutral English, exactly: "Cap." Pause, then repeat "Cap." Keep the final consonant audible without exaggerating the lips.
00:06–00:12 — Lower it, raise the cat card and say exactly: "Cat." Pause, then repeat "Cat."
00:12–00:18 — Hold both cards apart and say exactly: "Cap. Cat." Look from the correct card to the learner-facing camera after each word.
AUDIO: Clear dry voice, slight paper movement, no background music.
LOCK: Same teacher, two cards, card-to-word mapping, seated posture and camera position.
TEXT: None; add accessibility captions after checking the audio.
AVOID: swapped illustrations, extra words, comic accent, distorted mouth, automatic subtitles, new cards, logos or watermark.
```

## L04 — What the fragment tells us

**Use:** museum evidence and uncertainty · **Mode:** image to video  
**Input:** licensed reference of one broken pottery fragment  
**Format:** 16:9, 20 seconds · **Adjust:** highlight position only from verified visible features.

```text
Begin with the approved reference of a single ceramic fragment on a neutral padded support. Keep its chips, glaze, thickness and missing edges exactly as supplied.
00:00–00:06 — Camera moves through a small lateral arc to reveal the existing surface texture. Do not reconstruct the missing vessel.
00:06–00:13 — Focus shifts from one visible tool mark to the exposed ceramic edge. The fragment never turns or changes its support.
00:13–00:20 — Camera returns to a stable framing with empty space beside the object.
NARRATION: Exact English: "A fragment preserves some evidence and leaves other questions open. Missing parts are not permission to invent a history."
AUDIO: Quiet gallery ventilation below the narration. No mystical music or artifact ringing.
LOCK: Object size, support, crack pattern and lighting; no new markings.
AVOID: invented origin, dates, owners, ceremonies, restored missing parts, glowing highlights, generated provenance labels, logos or watermark.
```

## L05 — Same volume, different container

**Use:** observation and conservation of liquid · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 20 seconds · **Adjust:** safe colored water, no numerical measurement claim.

```text
A wide transparent cup contains a small amount of pale-blue water. An empty tall narrow cup stands beside it on a level classroom tray. Both are plain and unmarked.
00:00–00:06 — Fixed side view includes both cup bases and rims. An adult points at the lower water level in the wide cup.
00:06–00:14 — Pour all the water carefully into the tall cup without spilling. Show the continuous stream and the source level falling as the receiving level rises.
00:14–00:20 — Set the empty wide cup beside the tall one. Hold their visibly different water heights.
NARRATION: Exact English: "The container changed shape. We moved the water without adding any."
AUDIO: Pour and gentle cup contact under clear speech; no score.
LOCK: Two cups, one continuous transfer, no leaking or invisible replenishment.
AVOID: filling beyond the available volume, extra liquid, changing cup width, drinking, invented scale markings, labels, logos or watermark.
```

## L06 — One instruction at a time

**Use:** clear instructional video design · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 18 seconds · **Adjust:** paper color; keep the three-step sequence.

```text
Demonstrate folding one square sheet into a simple triangle, then opening it to inspect the crease. Fixed overhead framing shows the complete paper and both adult hands.
00:00–00:06 — Place the square flat, align opposite corners and bring one corner toward the other. English narration exactly: "Bring the corners together."
00:06–00:12 — Match the corners, hold them steady and smooth the fold once from center to edge. Say exactly: "Press along the fold."
00:12–00:18 — Open the same sheet and flatten it gently. Say exactly: "Open it and find the crease." Hold the diagonal line visible under side light.
AUDIO: Natural paper sounds below the narration; no music.
LOCK: One square sheet, one diagonal crease, unchanged color and overhead perspective.
TEXT: None.
AVOID: extra folds, cutting tools, impossible fingers, rotating the table, rapidly overlapping instructions, duplicated paper, captions, logos or watermark.
```
