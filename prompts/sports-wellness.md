# FLUX 3 sports and wellness prompts

Six low-risk movement and community briefs. They are not coaching qualifications, medical guidance or real performance evidence. Obtain consent for athlete and voice references.

<a id="s01"></a>
## S01 — Set the court together

**Use:** recreational sports community story · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 18 seconds · **Adjust:** clothing, keeping all activity before play.

```text
Two adult friends prepare a small indoor badminton court. A net is already installed correctly. One carries two rackets; the other holds one shuttlecock. No game begins.
00:00–00:06 — Locked wide view. The friends approach opposite sides of the net at a walking pace, staying clear of support posts.
00:06–00:12 — The first person passes one racket handle-first around the end of the net. The second sets the shuttlecock on an existing bench.
00:12–00:18 — Both step back, notice a stray bottle beside the court and move it onto the bench before returning to their positions.
AUDIO: Shoes on gym floor, gentle racket contact, bottle set-down and room echo. No dialogue, cheering or music.
LOCK: Two rackets, one shuttlecock, one bottle, stable net and court lines.
AVOID: gameplay, unsafe net adjustment, extra equipment, morphing rackets, exaggerated athletic bodies, brands, captions or watermark.
```

## S02 — The walk after the rain

**Use:** everyday outdoor movement editorial · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 2:1, 20 seconds · **Adjust:** park details without health claims.

```text
An adult with short gray hair, a muted red jacket and ordinary walking shoes follows a flat park path after light rain. Natural body and skin, no fitness-transformation narrative.
00:00–00:07 — Side tracking view at an easy walking pace. Arms swing naturally and shoes make normal contact with the damp path.
00:07–00:14 — The walker approaches a shallow puddle, slows and takes a small safe step around its edge. Camera slows with them rather than surging forward.
00:14–00:20 — They stop beside a bench, look at the trees and take a relaxed breath. Hold without posing for the camera.
AUDIO: Footsteps, light jacket movement and distant birds. No heartbeat effect, coach or music.
LOCK: Same person, jacket, shoes, travel direction and wetness.
AVOID: weight-loss implication, recovery guarantee, running speed, heroic sweat, unsafe stepping, extra walkers entering close frame, text, logos or watermark.
```

## S03 — One bounce to follow

**Use:** simple sports-motion continuity test · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** ball color, not the action.

```text
One adult in a blue T-shirt stands still on an empty outdoor basketball half-court, holding one orange basketball at waist height. Fixed side medium-wide camera includes hands, ball and ground.
00:00–00:05 — Hold the starting pose. The player looks at the clear ground in front of them.
00:05–00:10 — Push the ball down once with the right hand. It contacts the ground once, rebounds and is caught with both hands below chest height. Show the complete uninterrupted path.
00:10–00:15 — Hold the caught ball and relax. No second bounce, shot or running.
AUDIO: One synchronized ground contact, shoe adjustment and quiet court ambience. No crowd, voice or music.
LOCK: One ball, one bounce, same hands, body scale and light.
AVOID: floating ball, extra bounce sounds, ball changing size, hand passing through it, trick-shot claims, court logos, graphics or watermark.
```

## S04 — Choose a comfortable seat

**Use:** inclusive community-class welcome · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 18 seconds · **Adjust:** room decor; keep participation voluntary.

```text
A bright community room contains a wide semicircle of sturdy chairs with clear aisles. Four adults of different ages enter at their own pace; one uses a manual wheelchair and joins an open space in the circle.
00:00–00:06 — Stable wide view. A facilitator in a plain blue shirt gestures toward the available places without touching anyone.
00:06–00:12 — Participants choose seats or the open space. The wheelchair user positions independently, with realistic wheel movement and clearance.
00:12–00:18 — Facilitator says in English, exactly: "Take the space that feels comfortable for you." Everyone settles naturally. No exercise demonstration follows.
AUDIO: Chairs, wheels, quiet clothing movement and clear speech. No inspirational music.
LOCK: Same four participants, chair layout, clear aisle and wheelchair geometry.
AVOID: unsolicited assistance, pity framing, miracle recovery, crowded turning space, new participants, medical claims, captions, logos or watermark.
```

## S05 — The towel at the end

**Use:** understated sports-club service moment · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 9:16, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** locker-room colors; preserve privacy.

```text
In a fictional fully clothed club reception area, one adult attendant stacks three clean folded towels on an open shelf. A casually dressed adult member approaches from the corridor. No changing area, showers or mirrors.
00:00–00:05 — Fixed waist-up view. Attendant aligns the three towels and looks toward the arriving member.
00:05–00:10 — Member points to the top towel. Attendant lifts that one and passes it across the counter.
00:10–00:15 — Member accepts it and leaves frame. Attendant straightens the two remaining towels. Hold the shelf.
AUDIO: Cloth movement, footsteps and quiet interior ambience. No dialogue or music.
LOCK: Three towels initially, two at the end, same counter and people.
AVOID: nudity, private-room visibility, extra towels, hygiene certification, membership offers, brand marks, generated copy or watermark.
```

## S06 — A pause between rounds

**Use:** recreational table-tennis editorial · **Mode:** image to video  
**Input:** approved first frame of two consenting players beside a table  
**Format:** 16:9, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** only small gestures within the source framing.

```text
Preserve the two adults, ordinary sportswear, paddles and table from the reference. Both are at rest between practice rounds; do not generate an unseen rally.
00:00–00:05 — One player places their paddle flat on the table, away from the net. The other continues holding the second paddle.
00:05–00:10 — The first player picks up the existing water bottle from a side bench and holds it, without drinking or approaching the other person.
00:10–00:15 — Both look toward the far end of the room and share a small nod. Remain at rest.
AUDIO: Paddle contact, bottle pickup and room ambience; no score or dialogue.
LOCK: Identity, table lines, paddle count, bottle and stable lighting.
AVOID: invented points or scores, competitive endorsement, sweating changes, extra ball, floating bottle, active exercise coaching, labels, logos or watermark.
```
