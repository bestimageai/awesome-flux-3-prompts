# FLUX 3 ecommerce and product prompts

Six product briefs with visible geometry and state control. Use authorized product references; generated demonstrations do not establish real specifications or performance.

## E01 — Two dividers, one place

**Use:** tool-free organizer assembly concept · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 20 seconds · **Adjust:** wood tone, not part count.  
**Localization:** canonical source for I18N-02.

```text
Show a small wooden desk organizer on a plain workbench. There are exactly three product parts: one rectangular base with two parallel slots and two identical upright dividers. An adult's hands wear no jewelry. Fixed overhead camera, soft daylight from the left.
00:00–00:05 — Show all three parts separated. The right hand picks up one divider; the left steadies the base.
00:05–00:11 — Slide that divider into the first slot until it sits flush. Insert the second divider into the second slot. No hidden cut and no additional fasteners.
00:11–00:16 — Place one closed blue notebook upright between the dividers. The notebook is a prop, not an extra organizer part.
00:16–00:20 — Withdraw both hands and hold the finished arrangement. The notebook stays supported without changing size.
AUDIO: Two soft wood contacts, paper on wood and quiet room tone. No dialogue or music.
TEXT: None.
LOCK: Same three parts, two slots, wood grain and notebook throughout.
AVOID: self-assembly, extra dividers, disappearing fingers, new screws, invented load claims, text, logos or watermark.
```

## E02 — A clasp in plain view

**Use:** jewelry construction detail · **Mode:** image to video  
**Input:** approved macro first frame with bracelet unclasped  
**Format:** 1:1, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** finish before producing the reference.

```text
Animate one unbranded chain bracelet from the supplied reference. Preserve link count where visible, brushed metal, one lobster clasp and one end ring. A pair of adult hands is already positioned in frame.
00:00–00:05 — Locked macro angle. The right thumb opens the existing clasp lever while the left hand brings the end ring closer.
00:05–00:10 — Guide the ring into the open clasp and release the lever once. Show actual contact; do not hide the connection behind a fingertip.
00:10–00:15 — Lower the closed bracelet onto the matte cloth in a gentle oval. Hands leave; hold the clasp sharp while the far links fall softly out of focus.
AUDIO: Quiet metal contact and cloth friction; no sparkle effects, speech or music.
LOCK: Same clasp geometry, link thickness, scale and light direction. No precious-metal purity claim.
AVOID: fused links, disappearing ring, clasp switching sides, elastic metal, gemstone additions, generated prices, labels, logos or watermark.
```

## E05 — A handle that stays a handle

**Use:** ceramic mug product detail · **Mode:** image to video  
**Input:** approved first frame with one mug resting on a table  
**Format:** 1:1, 12 seconds · **Adjust:** camera arc, not product dimensions.

```text
Animate the reference of a single handmade mug with a broad C-shaped handle and a narrow darker glaze band below the rim. No other objects.
00:00–00:04 — Camera begins at the exact reference angle and moves slowly through a twenty-degree horizontal arc. Keep the mug fixed to the tabletop.
00:04–00:08 — Continue just far enough to reveal the handle's inner opening and its two attachment points. Focus follows the near edge without changing focal length.
00:08–00:12 — Decelerate to a stable three-quarter view and hold. Natural glaze variations remain visible.
AUDIO: Nearly silent studio ambience. No ceramic ringing without contact, no voice or music.
LOCK: Same handle gap, wall thickness, glaze band, imperfections, rim ellipse and contact shadow.
AVOID: floating mug, turning handle, extra handle, liquid appearing, exaggerated lens distortion, invented volume or dishwasher claims, text, logo or watermark.
```

```text
00:00–00:05 — Hold a complete side view, leaving enough room around the shoe for a later square crop.
00:05–00:11 — A slow camera push approaches the existing heel stitching while the shoe remains still. Do not turn it or reveal an invented unseen logo.
00:11–00:15 — Hold the close detail. Keep stitch spacing and material texture stable as focus settles.
AUDIO: Quiet studio only; no running footsteps, athletic voice-over or music.
LOCK: Shoe orientation, sole geometry, lace count where visible and platform shadow.
TEXT: Generate none; perform actual crop and layout work after generation.
AVOID: extra shoe, elastic sole, fabricated cushioning claims, fake motion blur, sports endorsement, label invention or watermark.
```
