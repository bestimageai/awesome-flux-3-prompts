# FLUX 3 travel and hospitality prompts

Six destination and service concepts. Use actual licensed references for a real property; synthetic scenes cannot verify access, dimensions, live conditions or amenities.

<a id="t01"></a>
## T01 — The room from the doorway

**Use:** restrained accommodation preview · **Mode:** image to video  
**Input:** approved doorway photograph of the actual room  
**Format:** 16:9, 18 seconds · **Adjust:** camera travel within visible geometry.

```text
Start on the supplied doorway view. Preserve room size, bed position, window, furniture, wall color and visible outdoor view. Do not invent anything behind the camera or through a closed door.
00:00–00:06 — Move the camera slowly forward no more than a small step, at natural eye height. Keep both doorposts outside the lens path.
00:06–00:12 — Pan gently toward the existing window while retaining part of the bed as a spatial anchor. A sheer curtain moves slightly in a soft breeze.
00:12–00:18 — Stop and hold the window, chair and bed in a balanced frame. No room service arrives.
AUDIO: Modest room ambience and curtain movement; no invented ocean, birds or luxury-hotel music.
LOCK: Same furniture count, window proportions, light direction and straight verticals.
AVOID: enlarged space, new balcony, extra amenities, wide-angle distortion, disappearing walls, occupancy claims, booking copy, logos or watermark.
```

## T02 — Ask before the portrait

**Use:** respectful travel-creator interaction · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 9:16, 20 seconds · **Adjust:** generic workshop craft; keep consent explicit.

```text
Create a fictional encounter at a small pottery stall. One adult potter works at a table; an adult visitor holds a phone lowered at waist level. Use ordinary contemporary clothes, no tourist costume stereotypes.
00:00–00:06 — Stable two-shot. The visitor asks in natural Spanish, exactly: "¿Puedo grabar tus manos mientras trabajas?" The phone remains lowered.
00:06–00:12 — Potter looks up and answers exactly: "Sí, solo las manos, por favor." Visitor nods and raises the phone only after the answer.
00:12–00:20 — Cut to a close view of the potter's hands smoothing the rim of one small bowl. Exclude the face and other stall visitors, honoring the stated boundary.
AUDIO: Two clear Spanish lines, quiet stall ambience and fingers on clay. No narration or score.
LOCK: Same bowl, tools, sleeves and table between shots.
AVOID: filming before consent, including face in the final view, extra dialogue, exoticized color grade, visible shop name, captions or watermark.
```

## T03 — The threshold, shown clearly

**Use:** access-detail illustration · **Mode:** image to video  
**Input:** approved photograph of a real entrance and its threshold  
**Format:** 16:9, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** camera crop; never redesign the entrance.

```text
Use the entrance photograph exactly. Preserve the threshold, door width, paving, slope and any existing step. Do not add a ramp or remove a barrier.
00:00–00:05 — Hold a natural standing-height view showing the full entrance.
00:05–00:10 — Camera tilts slowly downward to show the transition from approach surface to threshold. Keep the door jamb as a continuous spatial reference.
00:10–00:15 — Hold the lower view without measuring overlays or a person attempting entry.
NARRATION: Exact English: "Look closely at the entrance. Confirm dimensions and current access information with the venue before you travel."
AUDIO: Quiet actual-type street ambience beneath the voice; no uplifting score.
LOCK: All architecture, material joins and surface levels from the source.
AVOID: invented clearance, accessibility certification, altered slope, missing step, wheelchair demonstration from insufficient references, labels, logos or watermark.
```

## T04 — A map folded for the guest

**Use:** hospitality service moment · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 18 seconds · **Adjust:** desk materials; keep the map fictional and unreadable.

```text
At a modest guesthouse reception desk, one staff member in a plain gray shirt helps an adult guest carrying a small canvas bag. A folded paper map is the only document.
00:00–00:06 — Medium two-shot. Staff unfolds the map on the desk and indicates a short route with one finger. No map labels are readable.
00:06–00:12 — The guest follows the same line with their eyes and asks in English, exactly: "Can I walk there?" Staff answers exactly: "Let me check today's route first."
00:12–00:18 — Staff leaves the map open and reaches for an existing closed laptop, without displaying any result. End before advice is given.
AUDIO: Paper, two quiet lines and reception ambience; no music.
LOCK: One map, one laptop, same people, gestures and desk.
AVOID: invented travel times, safety guarantees, false live information, map text, branded tourism graphics, extra guests or watermark.
```

## T05 — The bag before the departure

**Use:** luggage-storage service concept · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 9:16, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** suitcase color, retaining one identifiable bag.

```text
A fictional small hotel lobby has one luggage rack behind a service counter. One adult guest places a green hard-shell cabin suitcase upright beside the counter.
00:00–00:05 — Fixed medium view. Guest retracts the existing telescopic handle and keeps a hand on the suitcase.
00:05–00:10 — Staff member waits for the guest's nod, then lifts the case by its top handle and places it on the rack without dragging it across the counter.
00:10–00:15 — Staff gives the guest one blank claim card. Guest accepts it, then exits frame. Hold on the stored case.
AUDIO: Handle click, wheels, paper card and lobby room tone. No speech or music.
LOCK: Green shell, four wheels, handle shape, one case and one card.
AVOID: fabricated storage policy, free-service promise, visible guest data, disappearing bag, physical strain, extra luggage, logos or watermark.
```

## T06 — The view that is really there

**Use:** honest destination framing · **Mode:** image to video  
**Input:** authorized photograph from the actual viewpoint  
**Format:** 2:1, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** subtle camera motion, never the destination.

```text
Animate the supplied landscape photograph without upgrading the weather or scenery. Keep all visible buildings, paths, vegetation, horizon and obstructions exactly where they are.
00:00–00:05 — Hold the original composition; allow only gentle movement in existing leaves or water.
00:05–00:10 — Make a small slow lateral camera move, staying within what the source can plausibly support. Do not expose a previously unseen panorama.
00:10–00:15 — Settle into a stable frame with the same foreground obstruction still present. Preserve the actual visibility conditions.
AUDIO: Only unobtrusive ambience consistent with visible surroundings; no invented waterfall, wildlife or traffic event.
LOCK: Landscape profile, season, clouds, architecture and viewpoint height.
TEXT: None; add a truthful location caption externally after verification.
AVOID: removing buildings, replacing sky, adding landmarks, perfect sunsets, travel guarantee, location labels, logos or watermark.
```
