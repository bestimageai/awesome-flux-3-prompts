# FLUX 3 advertising and UGC prompts

Six campaign concepts with specific product actions. Synthetic demonstrations are design visualizations, not evidence of real performance or customer experience.

<a id="a01"></a>
## A01 — Light through linen

**Use:** homeware campaign concept  
**Mode:** image to video · **Format:** 9:16, 15 seconds  
**Input:** an approved first frame showing the lamp and tabletop  
**Adjust:** lamp finish only before preparing the reference.

```text
Animate the supplied first frame of one unbranded table lamp: pleated cream linen shade, cylindrical terracotta base and one visible black cable. Preserve its exact shape, pleat count and scale.
00:00–00:05 — Camera makes a very slow ten-degree lateral move. Afternoon light grazes the shade; the lamp itself is off. Keep the room and cable stationary.
00:05–00:10 — One adult hand enters from the lower edge and operates the existing inline switch once. Warm light begins inside the shade, with realistic transmission through linen. Exposure changes gradually.
00:10–00:15 — The hand exits along the same path. Camera eases to a stop at the approved three-quarter view. Leave the upper background clear for copy added later.
AUDIO: Quiet room, sleeve brushing the table, one small switch click. No electrical surge, music or voice.
LOCK: Same cable route, shade seam, contact shadow and tabletop texture.
AVOID: duplicated lamp, additional controls, glowing solid base, disappearing cable, energy-saving claims, generated text, logo or watermark.
```

## A02 — The pocket actually closes

**Use:** casual creator product demonstration  
**Mode:** text to video · **Format:** 9:16, 18 seconds  
**Adjust:** pouch color and everyday contents; retain the exact item count.

```text
One adult creator in a dark green T-shirt stands at an ordinary kitchen counter, demonstrating an unbranded navy zip pouch. Fixed phone framing includes torso, hands and counter.
00:00–00:05 — Show the empty open pouch beside three items: one folded cable, one house key and one lip balm. Creator says in natural English: "These are the things I keep losing."
00:05–00:12 — Place the three items inside separately, then close the existing zipper in one uninterrupted movement. Keep the pouch's modest thickness plausible; do not shrink its contents.
00:12–00:18 — Put the closed pouch in a jacket pocket already hanging from a chair. Creator pats the pocket once and says: "Now they leave together." Hold briefly on the jacket.
AUDIO: Untreated room tone, metal key, fabric and zipper. No background song or applause.
LOCK: Same hands, zipper orientation, three items, pouch size and chair position.
AVOID: hidden edits during packing, extra compartments, invented testimonials, price or capacity claims, subtitles, labels or watermark.
```

## A03 — The first spoonful

**Use:** tactile food advertisement  
**Mode:** text to video · **Format:** 1:1, 15 seconds  
**Adjust:** bowl and topping colors; keep the food physics believable.

```text
Create a close food-film concept around a shallow cream ceramic bowl containing thick tomato soup, one small basil leaf and a thin swirl of cream. A steel spoon rests on a folded linen napkin.
00:00–00:05 — Locked three-quarter macro view. An adult hand picks up the spoon by its handle. Steam rises gently, not as a dense cloud.
00:05–00:10 — The spoon enters at the near edge and draws one slow arc through the cream. The swirl breaks only where the spoon passes. Lift a small spoonful; one drop falls back into the same spot.
00:10–00:15 — The spoon exits upward. Focus returns to the ripple fading on the surface. Leave a clear, stable end frame.
AUDIO: Spoon touching ceramic, a quiet pour-like movement and subtle restaurant room tone. No chewing, speech or score.
LOCK: Bowl shape, basil location until fluid moves it, consistent light from frame left and plausible liquid level.
AVOID: stretching soup, solid cream ribbons, endless steam, duplicate utensils, generated slogan, menu prices, logo or watermark.
```

<a id="a04"></a>
## A04 — One-handed opening, slowly

**Use:** product interaction study for a reusable lunch box  
**Mode:** image to video · **Format:** 16:9, 20 seconds  
**Input:** approved tabletop frame with the actual closed product  
**Adjust:** contents after verifying real compartment dimensions.

```text
Use the reference as the exact first frame. One rectangular lunch box has two visible side clips and a separate silicone seal in its lid. This is a concept demonstration, not a leakproof test.
00:00–00:06 — Locked overhead shot. One hand releases the left clip; the other releases the right. Each clip remains attached to its hinge.
00:06–00:12 — Lift the lid vertically and place it beside the box, underside visible. Reveal three compartments already filled with ordinary lunch portions; do not generate steam.
00:12–00:17 — Remove one carrot stick from the front compartment. Nothing else changes position.
00:17–00:20 — Hold the open layout, leaving space for post-production annotations.
AUDIO: Two distinct latch clicks, soft lid contact and room tone. No voice, score or exaggerated suction.
LOCK: Match geometry, clip count, material finish, seal and food quantity.
AVOID: self-opening hardware, extra hinges, bending plastic, magical freshness, unsupported claims, text, brand marks or watermark.
```

## A05 — Repair is the reveal

**Use:** repair-service campaign visualization  
**Mode:** text to video · **Format:** 9:16, 20 seconds  
**Adjust:** fabric and patch shape; keep the repair visibly modest.

```text
Show one worn blue canvas tote on a sewing table. A small rectangular tan patch is already pinned over a tear. Only an adult repairer's hands and forearms appear.
00:00–00:06 — Medium overhead view. Fingers flatten the patch and guide a threaded needle through the first visible stitch. Keep the repair small enough for the frame.
00:06–00:14 — Cut to a close side view after an explicit time jump: most of the seam is now complete. Show the final two stitches and a tied thread. Do not imply the full repair took seconds.
00:14–00:20 — Return to overhead. The repairer removes the pins into a tray, turns the tote upright and rests a hand beside the still-visible patch.
AUDIO: Needle through canvas, pin contacts and table creak. No narration or music.
LOCK: Same tear location, weave, patch, stitch color and handles. The bag remains used, not restored to a new product.
AVOID: unsafe needle placement, disappearing wear, invisible stitches, sustainability statistics, before-and-after proof claims, text, logos or watermark.
```

## A06 — The quiet last seat

**Use:** neighborhood venue campaign  
**Mode:** text to video · **Format:** 16:9, 18 seconds  
**Adjust:** venue colors; do not imply a real establishment or offer.

```text
Create a fictional neighborhood café scene just before opening. A staff member in a plain brown apron places the final chair at a small window table. No customers or readable signs.
00:00–00:06 — Static medium-wide view. The staff member lifts the wooden chair clear of the floor, turns it a quarter turn and lowers all four legs beside the table.
00:06–00:12 — Move only the camera a little closer as the staff member adjusts a folded napkin and one empty glass. Morning light traces the table edge.
00:12–00:18 — Staff member steps out of frame. Hold the empty welcoming seat with space around it. No one arrives and nothing fills itself.
AUDIO: Chair feet, folded cloth, distant coffee grinder from behind camera and quiet street ambience.
LOCK: Four chair legs, one glass, one napkin, window position and sunlight direction.
AVOID: free-food offers, reservation claims, branded interiors, extra furniture, artificial crowd noise, captions, promotional copy or watermark.
```
