# FLUX 3 multilingual audio prompts

Six short dialogue and sound briefs. Quoted lines below are intentionally in their assigned languages; directing instructions remain English. Language support does not guarantee accent accuracy or lip synchronization.

<a id="m01"></a>
## M01 — The repaired radio message

**Use:** intimate Mandarin dialogue · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 9:16, 18 seconds · **Adjust:** room details, keeping the short message.

```text
An adult daughter sits at a kitchen table beside a small repaired radio, recording a phone message for her father. Plain gray shirt, natural skin, ordinary afternoon light. Phone stays in her right hand.
00:00–00:06 — Locked medium close-up. She turns the radio's existing knob; a low clean tuning tone appears, not a recognizable broadcast or song.
00:06–00:13 — She speaks in natural Mandarin, exactly: "爸，收音机修好了。等你回来，我们再一起听。" Warm conversational delivery, one brief pause at the sentence break.
00:13–00:18 — She stops recording and places the phone beside the radio, then leaves the radio at a quiet level.
AUDIO: Subtle tuning noise, room tone and the one exact message. No reply from the phone.
LOCK: Same radio controls, hand, phone, shirt and table objects.
AVOID: extra dialogue, Cantonese substitution, subtitles, announcer voice, invented station names, famous music, exaggerated emotion, brands or watermark.
```

## M02 — A book returned in two languages

**Use:** bilingual turn-taking · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 18 seconds · **Adjust:** wardrobe, retaining each speaker's assigned language.

```text
Two adult neighbors meet in a simple apartment courtyard. Speaker A wears a blue jacket and holds one plain red paperback. Speaker B wears a cream jumper, hands empty.
00:00–00:06 — Stable medium two-shot. A offers the book and says in Spanish, exactly: "Gracias por prestármelo. Me gustó el final."
00:06–00:12 — B waits until A has finished, takes the book and replies in French, exactly: "Moi aussi. On en parle demain ?"
00:12–00:18 — A nods and says in Spanish, exactly: "Mañana, sin falta." Both stay in frame, relaxed.
AUDIO: Distinct natural voices, mild courtyard reverb, paper contact and distant outdoor ambience. No music.
LOCK: A speaks only Spanish; B speaks only French. Preserve voice identity, eyelines, one book and its handoff.
AVOID: translating either line, overlapping dialogue, random language switching, duplicate book, subtitles, dramatic gestures, readable title, logo or watermark.
```

## M03 — A tune for one window

**Use:** original Korean vocal excerpt · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 2:1, 20 seconds · **Adjust:** original melody, keeping lyrics and single performer.

```text
One adult singer in a plain navy shirt stands beside an open apartment window at dusk. No instruments or audience. Natural intimate performance, not imitation of any real singer.
00:00–00:05 — Medium profile; the singer breathes in while looking toward the window.
00:05–00:14 — Sing exactly once in Korean: "창가에 남은 작은 빛 / 오늘도 나를 기다려". Treat the slash as a brief line break, not a spoken symbol. Use a simple newly composed melody, quiet and unaccompanied.
00:14–00:20 — Let the final note fade and hold a natural relaxed expression. The camera barely moves.
AUDIO: One original vocal, soft room reflections and low distant traffic. No instrument added off screen.
LOCK: Voice identity, face, posture, window light and lyrical order.
AVOID: familiar song melody, extra lyrics, heavy tuning, English ad-libs, impossible sustained breath, subtitles, music-video effects, logos or watermark.
```

## M04 — Pass the blue folder

**Use:** multilingual sequence with separate contexts · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 20 seconds · **Adjust:** office decor, keeping speakers and languages distinct.

```text
Create three editorial shots connected by a blue folder handoff gesture. They are separate fictional workplaces, not one continuous room.
00:00–00:06 — In a modest office, an adult passes a blue folder to a colleague and says in Brazilian Portuguese, exactly: "Pode deixar aqui, por favor."
00:06–00:13 — Match cut to a different office. A different adult lowers a blue folder onto a desk and says in German, exactly: "Ich schaue es mir gleich an."
00:13–00:20 — Cut to a third office. Another adult closes a blue folder and says in Italian, exactly: "Ne parliamo domani." End on the closed folder.
AUDIO: Each room has its own quiet ambience. Keep speech turns separate, with natural local pronunciation. Paper sound motivates the cuts; no song.
LOCK: Stable identity within each shot; one folder per location. The offices and people must remain visibly distinct.
AVOID: translated captions, one person inheriting another voice, extra dialogue, mixed-language lines, logos, document text or watermark.
```

## M05 — The microphone distance

**Use:** clean speech and restrained movement test · **Mode:** image to video  
**Input:** approved portrait first frame with microphone already positioned  
**Format:** 16:9, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** background only before creating the reference.

```text
Preserve the consenting adult speaker and fixed tabletop microphone in the reference. Begin in a relaxed seated pose at the same mouth-to-microphone distance.
00:00–00:05 — Speaker looks toward the interviewer outside frame right, takes a natural breath and remains still.
00:05–00:10 — In standard Arabic, say exactly: "لنبدأ بخطوة صغيرة وواضحة." Clear conversational delivery; no announcer emphasis.
00:10–00:15 — Pause and give one small nod, keeping the microphone distance stable. No second sentence.
AUDIO: Dry intelligible Arabic voice with subdued room tone. No echo effect, music or additional speaker.
LOCK: Face, microphone location, cable route, voice, lighting and chair.
AVOID: unrequested dialect switching, translated words, lip exaggeration, head moving through microphone, subtitles, on-screen language labels, logos or watermark.
```

## M06 — Listen before the answer

**Use:** Japanese dialogue timing · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 18 seconds · **Adjust:** room materials, retaining the pause.

```text
Two adult colleagues sit across a small table with one closed laptop between them. Speaker A wears a brown cardigan; B wears a blue shirt. Natural indoor daylight, fixed medium two-shot.
00:00–00:06 — A asks in Japanese, exactly: "少し考えてもいいですか。" Keep hands resting beside the laptop.
00:06–00:12 — B nods once but waits three full seconds before answering exactly: "もちろん。急がなくて大丈夫です。"
00:12–00:18 — A relaxes their shoulders and looks down thoughtfully. Neither touches or opens the laptop. End in a comfortable silence.
AUDIO: Natural Japanese voices, quiet room ambience and slight clothing movement. No keyboard, notification, score or laugh track.
LOCK: Assigned speakers, one laptop, stable table positions and eyelines.
AVOID: overlapping lines, extra polite phrases, translation overlays, theatrical pauses, facial drift, new devices, captions, logos or watermark.
```
