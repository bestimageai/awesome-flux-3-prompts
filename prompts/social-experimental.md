# FLUX 3 social and experimental prompts

Six concepts for deadpan comedy, creator dialogue, original game worlds and editable production plates.

<a id="x01"></a>
## X01 — One useful sentence

**Use:** deadpan social short · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 9:16, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** clothing, not the pause or dialogue.  
**Localization:** canonical source for I18N-01.

```text
One adult facilitator in a gray jumper sits at a plain meeting table. A small tabletop microphone and a closed notebook are the only objects. Locked phone-camera medium shot; ordinary office lighting.
00:00–00:05 — The facilitator turns the microphone on with its existing button, looks toward the camera and says in calm English, exactly: "We have time for one useful sentence."
00:05–00:10 — Hold five full seconds of sincere silence. The facilitator glances once at the closed notebook but does not open it.
00:10–00:15 — Look back up and say exactly: "That was it." Switch the microphone off and remain seated.
AUDIO: Quiet ventilation, two small button clicks and natural speech. No music, laughter or audience reaction.
TEXT: None; no automatic subtitles.
LOCK: One person, one microphone, one notebook; same face, hands, clothing and framing.
AVOID: extra dialogue, exaggerated acting, opening the notebook, new attendees, fake company branding or watermark.
```

<a id="x02"></a>
## X02 — The rehearsal before the question

**Use:** two-person creator conversation · **Mode:** image to video  
**Input:** one approved first frame containing both consenting speakers and the set  
**Format:** 16:9, 20 seconds · **Adjust:** topic after rewriting both lines coherently.

```text
Preserve both adults, their clothes and the small recording table from the reference. Two microphones are fixed on stands; no additional equipment appears.
00:00–00:06 — The host takes a breath and asks in natural English, exactly: "Can I try the question out loud first?" Keep eye contact with the guest rather than the camera.
00:06–00:13 — The guest nods, waits a beat and replies exactly: "That was already a good one." The host releases a small surprised laugh.
00:13–00:20 — Both settle. The host squares a single blank note card on the table, then looks back to the guest. End before the interview begins.
AUDIO: Dry conversational voices, one small laugh, card contact and treated room ambience. No intro music.
LOCK: Two faces, microphone distance, cable routes, card and believable eyelines.
AVOID: invented biography, speaking over each other, extra lines, reaction zooms, fake waveform, subtitles, brands or watermark.
```

## X03 — The ferry of folded maps

**Use:** original game-concept teaser · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 16:9, 18 seconds · **Adjust:** miniature palette; preserve the stated mechanic.

```text
Create a stylized isometric concept for an original puzzle game. A small paper ferry carries one folded map across a shallow ink-blue channel between cardboard islands. There are no characters from existing games.
00:00–00:06 — Fixed isometric camera. The ferry approaches a gap between two islands; its movement leaves a restrained drawn wake.
00:06–00:12 — The map unfolds into a narrow paper bridge only after the ferry touches the near island. Keep folds readable and preserve the paper's original total area.
00:12–00:18 — One tiny wooden traveler steps across the new bridge and stops on the far island. The camera rises slightly to suggest another channel beyond.
AUDIO: Paper creases, gentle water, three wooden foot taps and an original three-note plucked motif.
LOCK: One ferry, one map, one traveler and consistent scale.
TEXT: None; a title may be added in post.
AVOID: recognizable franchise imagery, invented gameplay interface, collectibles, combat, endless paper, extra travelers, logos or watermark.
```

<a id="x04"></a>
## X04 — A clean product handoff plate

**Use:** compositing source concept · **Mode:** image to video  
**Input:** approved frame of consenting presenter, neutral clothing and blank box  
**Format:** 16:9, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** background hue before preparing the input.

```text
Use the reference of a waist-up presenter against evenly lit chroma green, holding one small blank matte-gray box. Keep complete hands and box inside the frame with generous edge clearance.
00:00–00:04 — Hold still at the exact reference pose. Presenter looks at the box, not the camera.
00:04–00:10 — Extend both arms gently toward frame right, offering the box into empty space. Stop before it leaves the image. Do not create a receiving person or hand.
00:10–00:15 — Hold the offered position with a natural slight breathing motion. Keep the box front blank for later compositing.
AUDIO: Clean sleeve movement and quiet studio tone. No speech or music.
LOCK: Identity, box dimensions, clothing and camera; consistent green background without hard shadows.
AVOID: cropped fingers, green spill on face, box rotation hiding its front, new hands, product labels, background texture, logos or watermark.
```

## X05 — A loop that returns its objects

**Use:** seamless-loop planning exercise · **Mode:** start/end to video  
**Input:** the same authorized still supplied as both endpoints  
**Format:** 1:1, 12 seconds · **Adjust:** surface color, not object count.

```text
Start and finish on the provided still of one wooden bead at the top of a shallow circular groove in a clay dish. The camera is locked overhead. This is a designed animation loop, not perpetual motion.
00:00–00:04 — The bead begins rolling clockwise along the groove, accelerating gently. Keep contact with the groove and its single shadow.
00:04–00:08 — Continue the same circuit through the bottom half. The dish remains absolutely stationary.
00:08–00:12 — Complete exactly one revolution, decelerating into the original position and orientation. Reach the supplied end frame without a dissolve.
AUDIO: Quiet rolling texture follows the bead and fades as it stops. No impact at the loop boundary, voice or music.
LOCK: One bead, one continuous groove, fixed dish and light.
AVOID: extra revolution, jumping bead, reversing direction, changed dish shape, hidden crossfade, text, symbols or watermark.
```

## X06 — The sound arrives from outside

**Use:** off-screen space and reaction test · **Mode:** text to video  
**Format:** 9:16, 15 seconds · **Adjust:** room style, retaining the unseen sound source.

```text
One adult illustrator in a striped shirt sits drawing at a kitchen table. A closed cupboard is visible behind them. Keep the drawing angled away and unreadable.
00:00–00:05 — Locked medium shot. The illustrator draws quietly with a pencil. A single soft ceramic clink sounds from outside frame left, not from the cupboard.
00:05–00:10 — They stop drawing and look left toward the sound, pausing with the pencil above the same sheet.
00:10–00:15 — A second identical clink sounds. The illustrator sets the pencil down carefully, smiles a little and remains seated. Never reveal what caused the sound.
AUDIO: Pencil, two spatially consistent clinks and ordinary room ambience. No dialogue or musical cue.
LOCK: One person, same paper and pencil, cupboard closed and camera fixed.
AVOID: object moving without cause, opening cupboard, appearing pet, extra sounds, reaction graphics, captions, logos or watermark.
```
